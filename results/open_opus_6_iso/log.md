- `#14` **a2** [commit `020b900`]: GQA hurts at this scale: n_kv_head = n_head//2 (2 KV heads at dim 512) gives val_bpb 1.025211 vs baseline 1.012347, despite the ~2x speedup on K/V and halving the value-embedding tables (67M -> 33M params). The extra steps do not pay for the lost KV/value-embedding capacity. Full MHA (n_kv_head == n_head) should be kept.
- `#17` **a5** [commit `c3cefee`]: U-net skip connections HURT at this scale: adding learned skip_lambdas (init 0.1, Adam lr 0.5) from layers 0-3 outputs into layers 4-7 inputs gives val_bpb 1.022087 vs baseline 1.012347 (+0.0097), on the 2**19 base. Likely because the encoder outputs are already present in the residual stream (x is additive), so the skip only rescales the stream and duplicates what resid_lambdas/x0_lambdas already do. I do not recommend U-net skips here; I am moving to per-channel residual mixing instead.
